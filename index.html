Privacy policy for Mythic Bricks

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Mythic Bricks ("the game") is published by Off Hours Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

The short version

  • We do not run any servers for the game and we do not collect, see or store your data ourselves.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • The game shows advertisements through Google AdMob. Google collects some data to deliver those ads, as described below.
  • There are no accounts, and no analytics or crash-reporting services other than what is part of Google AdMob.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, balls, cards, talents, research, achievements, daily streak) and your settings are saved only on your own device, in the game's private storage. This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or use the "Reset save" option in Settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. There are two kinds: ads you choose to watch in return for an in-game boost, and an occasional full-screen ad after a boss level.

To deliver and measure these ads, Google's advertising software inside the game may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other online identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • information about how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information from the advertising software.

This data goes directly from your device to Google. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you; we only see totals such as how many ads were shown. Google may process this data outside your country, including in the United States. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your choice about personalised advertising the first time it starts. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.
  • Changing your mind. Where that choice applies, you can change it at any time in the game under Settings → "Ad privacy choices".
  • On your device. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads), and manage ad personalisation at Google's Ads Settings.

Notifications

If you switch them on, the game schedules reminders (for example when research finishes). These are created and delivered entirely on your device and involve no server.

Permissions

The game uses internet access and the advertising ID for ads, and, if you enable reminders, permission to show notifications and schedule them, including after the device restarts. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or files outside its own storage.

Purchases

This version has no purchases. If optional purchases are added later, they will be handled by Google Play; Google, not the game, processes payment information. This policy will be updated before any such feature is released.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Legal basis and your rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Showing non-personalised ads and keeping the game working rely on our legitimate interest in funding and operating a free game. You have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data about you ourselves, requests about advertising data are best made to Google through the links above; you can also contact us and we will help where we can.
